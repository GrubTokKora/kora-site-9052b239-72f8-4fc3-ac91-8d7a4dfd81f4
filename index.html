About Taco John's

Taco John's is an American fast food restaurant serving Mexican-inspired fast food. We're famous for our signature dish, Potato Olés®, which are bite-sized deep-fried potato nuggets coated with a proprietary blend of seasonings. We're committed to quality ingredients and bold flavors that you won't find anywhere else.
