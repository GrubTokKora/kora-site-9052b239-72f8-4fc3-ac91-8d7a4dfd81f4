Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ordering, locations, and our Mexican menu.

Where are your Taco John's locations? +

We currently serve Leominster, MA (536 N. Main Street), Boston, MA (77 Summer Street), and a Newington, CT location coming soon at 56B Costello Road. Find hours, directions, and online ordering on each location page.

What Mexican food and menu favorites do you serve? +

Enjoy bold Mexican flavors including tacos, burritos, Potato Olés®, Stuffed Grilled Tacos, the Fried Chicken Taco, breakfast burritos, and everyday Value Menu options. Order online or visit us for breakfast, lunch, or dinner.

How do I order online or for delivery? +

Order now at order.tacojohns.com, use the Taco John's mobile app, or choose DoorDash for delivery. Join Bigger Bolder Rewards to earn free food.

Do you offer catering for groups and events? +

Yes—order catering for offices, parties, and events through our catering partners. Visit the Catering link in the menu for trays of tacos, burritos, and Potato Olés®.
